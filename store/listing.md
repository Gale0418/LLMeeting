## 繁體中文

### 短說明

在 Chrome 側邊欄召集 ChatGPT、Gemini、Claude、Grok、Meta AI 多回合互評與群聊！OWO+ 不用付費、免設 API 金鑰，直接使用已登入網頁版運作 ヾ(•ω•`)o

### 詳細說明

想要一次聽聽多個 AI 的想法，卻懶得在十幾個分頁間複製貼上嗎？歡迎來到 LLMeeting ヾ(•ω•`)o 這是一款住在 Chrome 側邊欄的多 AI 圓桌會議擴充功能，能直接協調你已登入的 ChatGPT、Gemini、Claude、Grok 與 Meta AI（Beta）網頁版，進行多回合交叉互評與可選的主席總結！OWO+ 不用付費即可使用所有會議功能，完全免設定 API 金鑰、免綁卡解鎖，直接使用你原本的各家 AI 網頁版就能立刻開會（各家 AI 網站本身的帳號登入、訂閱方案、用量額度與地區限制仍依各平台規定適用喔）。

✨ 四大會議模式，隨心切換節奏 (｀・ω・´)ゞ
• 快速鬥技場：各家 AI 同台過招，快速比較各方觀點與亮點。
• 自由群聊：像在聊天室一樣自在！你可以在等待你發言時插話、再互評、結案或停止。
• 劇場大亂鬥：搭配角色扮演與情境設定，看 AI 們飆戲過招。
• 總結辯論：深入交鋒並整理各方論點。除了自由群聊外，其他模式皆可自由選擇 1～5 輪交叉評析，並決定是否交由主席總結！

🎭 百變互動風格與趣味玩法
不只能進行嚴肅互評，還能切換酒吧閒聊、共識接龍、犀利吐槽、抓內鬼，以及匿名或化裝互評！你可以觀察不同模型的觀點分歧、玩角色扮演，或是一起猜內鬼等最後揭曉。雖然多方互評不保證消除 AI 幻覺或必然產出唯一最佳解答，但能幫你激盪出更豐富的靈感；重要事實仍請自行查證！

🛠️ 貼心順手的實用設計
• 接續既有對話：可明確指定已開啟的 AI 對話分頁，唯讀擷取該頁已載入的對話內容，加上你的補充說明作為「第 0 輪」背景，直接請其他 AI 接手討論（僅讀取畫面已載入內容，不會讀取未載入的隱藏歷史）。
• 故障暫停可補貼：遇到網頁卡頓或暫停時別擔心，可直接貼上 AI 網頁已產生的回答補齊缺段，讓會議順暢接續。
• 靈感草稿與多語切換：內建新手示範與劇場挑戰卡，點擊後只會填入草稿框，讓你修改滿意再送出；會議進行中也能隨時切換繁體中文、英文、日文介面（可跟隨瀏覽器或自選）。
• 隱私透明與免費彩蛋：會議紀錄保存在本機 Chrome storage，LLMeeting 絕不會把你的對話傳送到開發者伺服器（你的提問與互評內容僅會發送至你選擇參與會議的各家 AI 網站；本工具為獨立開發，與各 AI 官方無合作關係）。連可愛的羊羊彩蛋也純屬趣味裝飾，不解鎖付費項目，所有功能完全免費開放 ヾ(•ω•`)o

🔎 使用前先登入至少兩家支援的 AI 網站。會議紀錄在本機保留最多 24 小時；匿名玩法使用會議化名，不會隱藏你在 AI 網站的帳號身分。

Multi-AI peer review & group chat for ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok & Meta AI! OWO+ Free, no API keys—runs on your web tabs!

### 詳細說明

Tired of copy-pasting across tabs? LLMeeting ヾ(•ω•`)o coordinates your logged-in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, and Meta AI (Beta) web pages in the Chrome side panel for multi-round peer review and optional Chair summaries! OWO+ LLMeeting extension features are free to use, with no paid unlocks and no API keys needed—we work directly with your logged-in AI web tabs (each AI provider's own account login, subscription plan, usage quota, and regional restrictions still apply).

✨ 4 Meeting Modes & Playful Styles (｀・ω・´)ゞ
Pick Quick Arena, Free Group Chat (add a message when the meeting is waiting for you, then request peer review, wrap up, or stop), Theater Brawl, or Summary Debate. Outside Free Group Chat, choose 1–5 cross-review rounds with an optional Chair summary! Switch between Serious Peer Review, Bar Chat, Consensus Relay, Roast, Find the Mole, and Anonymous or Disguised review styles. Enjoy roleplay, compare where viewpoints diverge, or guess the mole before the final reveal (great for exploring perspectives, though it does not guarantee eliminating hallucinations or producing a single best answer).

🛠️ Practical & Transparent Features
• Existing Tab Capture: Explicitly select an open AI tab to read-only capture its loaded conversation plus your follow-up note as Round 0 for other AIs to discuss (captures only what is currently loaded on the page, not full hidden history).
• Easy Pause Recovery: If a round pauses due to a page hiccup, simply paste the reply already generated on the AI web page to fill in the missing segment and continue.
• Draft Cards & Live Trilingual UI: Starter demos and Theater challenge cards only pre-fill your draft box so you can edit before sending. Switch between Traditional Chinese, English, and Japanese anytime—even mid-meeting—following your browser or manual choice.
• Local Storage & Free Easter Egg: Meetings are saved locally in Chrome storage. LLMeeting never sends your chats to developer servers, though your prompts and peer-review texts are sent to the AI websites you select (independent tool with no official affiliation). Even the little sheep easter egg is purely decorative—every feature is completely free! ヾ(•ω•`)o

🔎 Sign in to at least two supported AI websites to begin. Meeting history is kept locally for up to 24 hours. Anonymous modes use aliases, not anonymous accounts; they do not hide your account identity from AI services. Peer review can surface differing views, but it does not guarantee accuracy; check important facts.

複数のAIタブを行き来してコピペするのに疲れていませんか？LLMeeting ヾ(•ω•`)o は、Chromeのサイドパネルからログイン済みの ChatGPT、Gemini、Claude、Grok、Meta AI（Beta）のWeb版を直接連携し、複数ラウンドの相互評価や任意の議長まとめを行える拡張機能です！OWO+ すべての会議機能が無料で使え、有料ロック解除やAPIキー設定も一切不要。普段使っているWeb版のまま直接動作します（各AIサービスのアカウント、利用枠、有料プラン、地域制限などは各社の規約に従います）。

✨ 4つの会議モードと多彩なスタイル (｀・ω・´)ゞ

## Test instructions

1. Install the unpacked extension.
2. Sign in to ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, and Claude in Chrome.
3. Optionally open Meta AI in a signed-in account; Meta AI Beta is not required for the stable four-provider flow.
4. Open the LLMeeting side panel from the toolbar icon.
5. Enter a short question such as `天為什麼是藍的？`.
6. Set 交叉評析輪次 to 2, run 快速鬥技場, and confirm every selected provider receives the initial prompt and two critique prompts. 裁判總結 sends the final prompt to one chair; 全員匿名 sends it to every participant independently.
7. Confirm Meta AI Beta is not enabled after a fresh install; activation buttons expose their state through aria-pressed.
8. Click 清除 (清除紀錄), confirm the operation, and verify the transcript returns to an empty local state without changing the selected participants.

## Permissions rationale

- `sidePanel`: show the LLMeeting control panel.
- `storage`: keep the latest local debate state for up to 24 hours and remember provider selections, UI language, and the local 🐑 mode easter egg preference.
- `tabs`: list provider tabs for an explicit connection choice, or open fresh provider tabs when none is selected.
- `scripting`: reinject the content script when a provider page is already open.
- Host permissions for ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, X Grok, Claude, and Meta AI: automate prompt entry and read replies only on explicitly supported AI web apps. Meta AI remains opt-in in the side panel.
