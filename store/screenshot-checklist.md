# Chrome Web Store Screenshot Checklist

Prepare at least these screenshots before submitting LLMeeting to the Chrome Web Store:

1. Side panel idle state with Free badge, four enabled stable providers, disabled Meta AI Beta, and the main 快速鬥技場 button.
2. The five-click sequence ending in the 🐑 badge, followed by Reset returning the badge to Free.
3. Advanced debate settings opened with all four meeting modes, four summary strategies, interaction styles, and 交叉評析輪次 controls. In free chat, show that round limits are hidden.
4. A running 快速鬥技場 with provider status cards showing 回答中 or 已回答.
5. Transcript view with first-round answers and cross-critiques.
6. Meta AI enabled in a signed-in supported account, with a successful text response. Do not add a Beta label to the five square model buttons; describe Beta availability in the listing instead.
7. Diagnostics panel expanded to show local provider state and a redacted Meta AI Beta result.
8. The local retention notice and 清除紀錄 control, followed by the empty state after clearing.

Recommended capture notes:

- Use a clean Chrome profile or hide personal chat history.
- Use short harmless prompts such as `天為什麼是藍的？`.
- Avoid exposing private account names, emails, or paid-plan information.
- Capture real extension runs only; do not submit mock-runtime screenshots as proof that a meeting succeeded.
- Match screenshots to the version being submitted. The repository candidate and current Store version may differ.
- Include a useful sample question, readable replies, and a failure-recovery view; do not invent testimonials, completion times, or accuracy claims.
